## Project Scope

This case study will examine Credit Acceptance through the economics of its dealer programs, loan performance, forecast methodology, collections, and capital allocation.

## Research Questions

- How do forecast collections translate into reported yield and profitability?
- Which disclosures best reveal changes in underlying credit performance?
- How should risk-adjusted returns be compared across vintages and cycles?
