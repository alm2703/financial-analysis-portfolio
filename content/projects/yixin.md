## Project Scope

This case study will map Yixin's transaction-platform and financing businesses, with special attention to risk management, unit economics, and market structure.

## Research Questions

- How do platform and financing revenues differ economically?
- What are the key risk and funding constraints in China's auto-finance market?
- Which comparisons with US lenders are valid, and which are misleading?
