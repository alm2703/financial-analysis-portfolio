## Start with the balance sheet

For a lender, the balance sheet is not merely a record of prior activity. It is the productive asset base, the funding structure, and a map of embedded risk.

## Separate volume, price, funding, and loss

Revenue growth can come from a larger portfolio or a higher yield. Earnings can still fall if funding costs or credit losses rise faster. A useful model keeps these effects separate.
